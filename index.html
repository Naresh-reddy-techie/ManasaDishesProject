Our story

Good food doesn’t need to be complicated. It just needs to be fresh.

We source our ingredients from a fresh local farm, where crisp cucumbers, tender greens, coconuts and wholesome produce are grown naturally and brought to us at their freshest. We turn those simple ingredients into food and drinks that feel fresh, colourful and comforting.

Our Ceci Bowl brings together fresh ingredients with two signature dressings, giving you two completely different ways to enjoy the same bowl. For drinks, we keep things naturally refreshing: a creamy coconut smoothie and a cool cucumber margarita.

We don’t hide freshness behind complicated recipes. We let the ingredients speak for themselves.

From a quiet morning at the farm to the bowl and glass on your table, this is our little journey from earth to plate, and from plate to happiness.
